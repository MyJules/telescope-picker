The extension bundle includes fuzzysort:
https://github.com/farzher/fuzzysort

Content search packages @vscode/ripgrep and its platform-specific binary:
https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-ripgrep
https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep
Their MIT license notices are included with the packaged node_modules.
Ripgrep is not taken from the VS Code installation or the user's PATH.
